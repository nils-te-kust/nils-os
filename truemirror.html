True Mirror ◐

nils os · meet yourself, from the side

A mirror lies twice. It flips you left–right, and it can never look back — when you look at your reflection's eyes, the camera sees you looking at the screen, not at yourself.

This shows your true, un-mirrored image — the face everyone else knows. And it tracks your eyes: the moment your gaze lands on the eyes of the person on screen, their eyes are redirected to meet yours.

1 · Allow the camera and sit at arm's length, face lit from the front.

2 · A 10-second calibration: look at the camera lens, then into your own on-screen eyes.

3 · Then just look. Add a bit of delay to break the sync — the person stops copying you and becomes someone you meet.
